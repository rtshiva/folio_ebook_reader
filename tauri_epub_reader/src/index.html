An EPUB reader for your books

Folio.

Folio is an EPUB reader that helps you read books comfortably — open any DRM-free .epub and it sets the text the way a good paperback would: type sized to your screen, a measure your eye can hold, leading with room to breathe. Wide windows open into a two-page spread on its stand, with bionic reading, dyslexia-tuned typefaces, and a guided line to help you keep reading.
